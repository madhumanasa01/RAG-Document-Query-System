# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may plan their own schedule as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Work on public holidays is compensated with a day off within the following 30 days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their probation period. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head and are reviewed every six months. The company reimburses up to 1,500 rupees per month for home internet.

## Leave policy
Every employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 6 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any remaining days lapse on 31 March. Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, and a medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days. Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Probation
New employees serve a probation period of six months. During probation the notice period is 15 days; after confirmation it becomes 60 days.

## Expenses
Travel must be booked through the internal travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with original receipts attached. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 1,200 rupees per day.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 40,000 rupees for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests above 15,000 rupees need manager approval in advance.

## Equipment
Engineers receive a laptop on their first day, which is refreshed every three years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
