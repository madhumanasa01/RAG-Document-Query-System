# Riverside Family Clinic: Patient FAQ

## Appointments
Appointments can be booked online, through the mobile app, or by calling the front desk between 8 am and 8 pm. Same-day appointments are released at 7 am every morning. Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to complete registration.

## Cancellations
Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance. Patients who miss three appointments without notice within a year may be asked to prepay for future bookings.

## Consultation fees
A general physician consultation costs 500 rupees and a specialist consultation costs 900 rupees. A follow-up visit within 7 days of the original consultation is free of charge.

## Lab tests
Blood samples are collected from 7 am to 11 am. Fasting tests such as lipid profile and fasting glucose require 10 to 12 hours without food; water is allowed. Most reports are available in the patient portal within 24 hours, while culture reports can take up to 72 hours.

## Vaccinations
The clinic offers routine childhood immunisations, annual influenza vaccines, and travel vaccines. Influenza vaccines are available from September to February. Travel vaccines should be taken at least four weeks before departure.

## Insurance
The clinic accepts cashless claims from twelve partner insurers. Patients must present their insurance card and a government photo ID at registration. Reimbursement claims for non-partner insurers require an itemised bill, which the billing desk provides on request.

## Parking and accessibility
Free parking is available in the basement for up to two hours. The building has ramps and an elevator, and wheelchairs can be borrowed from the reception.
