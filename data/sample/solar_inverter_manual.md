# SunGrid H5 Hybrid Inverter: Installation and Operation Manual

## Specifications
The SunGrid H5 is a 5 kW single-phase hybrid inverter. It accepts a maximum PV input voltage of 550 V across two MPPT trackers, each supporting up to 13 A of input current. The battery port works with 48 V lithium iron phosphate batteries and supports a maximum charge and discharge current of 100 A. Peak conversion efficiency is 97.6 percent.

## Installation
Mount the inverter vertically on a non-flammable wall, out of direct sunlight. Leave at least 50 cm of clearance above and below the unit for airflow. The operating temperature range is -25 °C to 60 °C; above 45 °C the inverter derates its output power automatically. The enclosure is rated IP65, so it can be installed outdoors under a canopy.

## Commissioning
Before switching on, confirm the PV string polarity with a multimeter. Turn on the battery breaker first, then the PV isolator, and finally the AC grid breaker. The display shows "Self-check" for about 60 seconds before the unit connects to the grid.

## Operating modes
In Self-Use mode, solar energy powers household loads first, surplus charges the battery, and only the remainder is exported. In Backup mode, the battery is kept above 80 percent state of charge to cover outages. Switchover to backup power during a grid failure takes less than 10 milliseconds.

## Error codes
E01 indicates PV over-voltage: reduce the number of panels in the string. E07 indicates grid frequency out of range and usually clears automatically. E12 means the battery communication cable is disconnected; check the CAN connection between the battery and the inverter. E21 is an over-temperature fault; improve ventilation around the unit.

## Warranty
The SunGrid H5 carries a standard warranty of 7 years from the date of installation, which can be extended to 12 years by registering the product online within 90 days. The warranty does not cover damage caused by lightning or by installation that does not follow this manual.
